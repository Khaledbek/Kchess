   Running search uses complete MultiPV rank 1 provisionally. Completed search

   Completed legal moves keep a normal native class. Stability/depth/budget

3. Progressive Depth Reclassification

4. SF18/SF19 Isolation

   live workers but keeps each engine's compatible persisted analysis. Position

   without unnecessary Stockfish work. Flutter performs the terminal adjacent

   behind the penultimate running snapshot.
