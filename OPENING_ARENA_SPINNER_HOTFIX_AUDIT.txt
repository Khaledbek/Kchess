KChess Opening Arena Spinner Hotfix 1/1

Cause:
- OpeningScenarioList issued a separate openingStatus practiceCommand before families/nodes.
- FfiCoreGateway.practiceCommand enters native synchronously on the Flutter UI isolate despite returning Future.
- A diagnostic preflight on first render can therefore prevent the page from reaching its normal catalogue/error state.

Fix:
- Remove openingStatus preflight from OpeningScenarioList._load().
- Load authoritative families/nodes directly.
- Preserve detailed CoreGatewayException rendering on the real catalogue/start operations.
- No KCL/KCO/KCB format or runtime topology changes.

Verification policy:
- Static audit only. No build/test/flutter analyze/flutter run executed.
